About Bal Jyoti

A school built on learning, values and opportunity

Bal Jyoti Public School was established in 1987 and is managed by the Krishna Gopal Samitee, Uttar Pradesh. Located on GT Road at Lal Kuan, near NH-24 in Ghaziabad, the school is a co-educational institution spread across a campus of approximately 8,855 square metres.

Affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), the school offers English-medium education from Nursery through Class XII. Our aim is to provide a supportive environment where academic learning goes hand in hand with creativity, discipline and community life.

Bal Jyoti means “the light of children.” We believe every child has the potential to grow, question, create and contribute. Our teachers guide students to build knowledge, confidence, good character and respect for others.

Academics

A strong foundation at every stage

Each stage of school builds the knowledge, skills and confidence students need for the next step.

Nursery – UKG

Pre-Primary

A warm, activity-based introduction to school through play, stories, rhymes, language, numbers and social learning.

Class I – V

Primary

Strong foundations in literacy and numeracy, alongside environmental studies, computers, art and physical education.

Class VI – VIII

Middle School

Wider subject learning in Science, Social Science and Mathematics, with growing independence and classroom participation.

Class IX – X

Secondary

Structured learning and assessment to support students through the CBSE secondary curriculum and board examinations.

Class XI – XII

Senior Secondary

Focused subject learning and preparation for board examinations and future higher-education choices, subject to school offerings.
