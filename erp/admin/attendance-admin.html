Upload or scan the daily attendance register, verify each employee record and publish attendance. Only hr@baljyoti.com can submit attendance for staff.

1. SalaryBox Attendance

Required columns: email, name, check_in, check_out, status. employee_id is recommended.

Take a photo of the daily signed attendance register or upload a scan.

OCR will read printed names. Handwritten time and absence marks need manual verification.
