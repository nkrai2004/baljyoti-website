Scan Paper Attendance Sheet

Take a photo with your phone camera or upload a clear image of the signed attendance sheet. The scanner will read printed teacher names and suggest matches against the ERP teacher register.

OCR runs in the browser after you select an image.

Important: handwriting, signatures and red crosses may be misread by OCR. The scan only suggests teacher-name matches. It will not mark anyone Present or Absent automatically. Review the original sheet and confirm each status in the Daily Register before saving.

Data handling: SalaryBox punch data is kept separate from the ERP attendance decision. Conflicts are marked for verification; imported values do not silently overwrite an existing manual decision.
